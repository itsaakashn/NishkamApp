# Delete Your Nishkam Account and Data

**App:** Nishkam — Bhagavad Gita (`com.nishkam.app`)  
**Developer:** The Aighth · theaighth@gmail.com

**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

---

## Option 1 — In the app (instant)

1. Open Nishkam and go to the **Settings** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account & data**.
3. Tap **Delete** to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Option 2 — Without the app (by email)

If you no longer have the app installed, email **theaighth@gmail.com** with the subject **"Nishkam account deletion"** from the Google account you signed in with. We will confirm within 7 days.

You can also remove Nishkam's access yourself at any time at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) → Nishkam → **Delete all connections**.

---

## What is deleted

- Your profile name and optional date of birth
- Reading progress, streak, bookmarks and "Carry This" cards
- All preferences (guidance path, language, reminder time, font)
- Nishkam's access grant to your Google account (revoked)

## What is kept

**Nothing.** Nishkam has no servers and no database — all of your data lives only on your device, and deletion erases all of it. There is no retention period. Your Google account itself is not affected; it is managed by Google.
